Whole Wheat Slider Buns

Yield: 8-10 buns (~55 g each)

Ingredients

  • 300 g whole wheat flour (100%)
  • 195 g warm water, ~105°F (65%)
  • 6 g active dry yeast (2%)
  • 21 g honey or maple syrup (7%)
  • 18 g olive oil or melted butter (6%)
  • 6 g salt (2%)

Instructions

  1. Whisk warm water, yeast, and honey. Rest 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Stir in flour, oil, and salt to form a shaggy dough.
  3. Knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Cover in a greased bowl and let double, 60-90 minutes.
  5. Divide into ~55 g balls. Place 2 inches apart on a parchment-lined sheet and press lightly to flatten.
  6. Cover loosely and rise 30-45 minutes. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) during the last 15 minutes.
  7. Optional: egg wash and sesame seeds. Bake 12-15 minutes until golden and hollow-sounding when tapped. Cool on a rack.

Vanilla cottage cheese waffle (they liked)

Yield: 2 servings / 8 waffles

Ingredients

  • 1 cup high-protein cottage cheese (200 g)
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2-1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (gluten-free) oats (2.4 dl)
  • 2 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.
  2. Spoon batter into a preheated, greased waffle iron and cook until golden, about 3 minutes per waffle.
  3. Store in an airtight container in the fridge.
  4. Reheat in the toaster for about 1 minute. Serve with berries, for example.

Rice dishes - Creamy Rice Bowl yt

Rice dishes

Macros: Entire recipe: 3376 calories, 316C, 106F, 215P. Per 1 of 5 servings: 675 calories, 63C, 21F, 43P.

Ingredients

  • 900 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 350 g arborio rice
  • 3 onions
  • 600 g mushrooms
  • 1000 g water + 15 g stock powder
  • 100 g Parmesan
  • 20 g olive oil
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 20 g rosemary
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into chunks. Toss with 10 g olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Chop onions and mushrooms, grate Parmesan, mix water with stock powder, and finely chop rosemary.
  3. Sear chicken on medium-high for 1 minute per side; remove.
  4. Cook mushrooms with a splash of water for 7 minutes until the water evaporates. Add 5 g olive oil and stir-fry.
  5. Add onions with 5 g olive oil and a pinch of salt; stir-fry 3 minutes.
  6. Add rice and rosemary and toast 1 minute. Add broth and chicken. Cover and simmer on medium-low 20-25 minutes.
  7. Check every 7-8 minutes and add stock if needed before the rice is cooked.
  8. Remove from heat, stir in Parmesan, and divide into 5 containers. Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze up to 3 months.

Pastas - Lasagna yt

Pastas

Veggies

  • 1 medium zucchini
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 1 medium red bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 shallot

Sauce

  • 3 x 15 oz (425 g) cans tomato sauce + 1/2 can water
  • 1 tsp (6 g) salt
  • 1 tsp (3 g) garlic powder
  • 2 tsp (6 g) onion powder
  • 1 tsp (3 g) black pepper
  • ~2 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • ~5 tsp dried basil

Meat

  • 2 lb (906 g) 93/7 ground beef
  • 1 tsp (6 g) salt

Pasta

  • 12 oz (340 g) oven-ready lasagna noodles

Cheese mix

  • 2 x 16 oz (454 g) containers 2% cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp (20 g) cornstarch
  • 2 large eggs
  • ~1 tbsp dried parsley
  • ~1 cup low-moisture part-skim mozzarella
  • ~1/2 cup Parmigiano Reggiano

Cheese topping

  • 1 lb (453 g) mozzarella, shredded
  • 5.25 oz (150 g) Parmigiano Reggiano, shredded
  • Dried parsley after baking

Instructions

  1. Dice vegetables to similar size; mince garlic and shallot separately. Heat oven to 400°F (204°C).
  2. Simmer sauce ingredients covered on low.
  3. Brown meat with salt; add garlic and shallot as it finishes. Add to sauce.
  4. Cook mushrooms first, then remaining vegetables until slightly softened. Add to sauce and turn off heat.
  5. Whisk or blend cottage cheese and cornstarch, then whisk in remaining cheese-mix ingredients.
  6. Build three layers of sauce/cheese mix with two noodle layers, finishing with shredded cheese.
  7. Tent with foil and bake 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake 15 minutes more.
  8. Raise oven to 450°F (230°C) and bake another 5-10 minutes to desired browning. Rest 15 minutes.

Sandwiches - Lentil burgers

Sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups dried red lentils
  • 2 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large shallot or 1 small onion, small dice (about 1/3 cup)
  • 1/3 cup grated carrots (about 3 small)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • Neutral-flavored cooking oil for pan frying, optional

Instructions

  1. Soak lentils in water by about 2 inches for 2 hours; rinse and drain thoroughly.
  2. For baking, preheat oven to 400°F and line a sheet with parchment or a silicone mat.
  3. Process drained lentils, garlic, shallot, carrot, tomato paste, cilantro, salt, and pepper until well combined and the mixture holds together.
  4. Scoop about 1/4 cup per patty and shape about 1/2 inch thick.
  5. Bake 10 minutes, flip, and bake about 10 more minutes, or until crisp on both sides.
  6. Or pan-fry in oil about 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp.

Different to try - Other Pizzas - Next to try yt

Different to try

Notes from source (2025-09-10): Worked well with half white flour and half whole-wheat einkorn. Fridge fermentation for 2 days. Leftover dough balls went to freezer after the 2-day fridge fermentation. Recipe gave 4 balls, each about 2 meals for him. If frozen dough works well, source notes it may be worth doubling and freezing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 2.5 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 1/4 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Combine warm water, instant yeast, and sugar; rest 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add milk, salt, olive oil, and flour. Mix at speed 2 for 3-4 minutes, then speed 4 for 3-4 minutes until smooth and elastic. By hand, knead 6-10 minutes.
  3. Cover and rest 10-20 minutes.
  4. Divide into 2-3 portions. For 16-17 inch thin crust, source divides into two ~409 g balls.
  5. Lightly oil a bowl/tray, coat dough balls in oil, cover, and rest 2-3 hours until doubled and easily stretchable.
